FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to the questions investors ask most. If you need more detail, our support team is available 24/7.

NOVEX operates as a structured investment ecosystem. Investors join defined investment cycles, contribute capital, and participate in distributions based on operational results. An AI-powered monitoring engine oversees all cycle activity, ensuring transparency and operational consistency throughout the process.

Investment cycles follow strict operational frameworks with predefined durations, parameters, and distribution schedules. Each cycle is governed by established protocols that remove subjective decision-making and ensure every investor is treated consistently within the same cycle conditions.

Profit distributions occur on scheduled weekly windows. Once a distribution event is triggered, funds are automatically allocated to investor wallets according to their participation level within the cycle. All distributions are recorded and visible in the investor dashboard.

Withdrawal requests can be initiated through your investor dashboard during designated withdrawal windows. The availability of funds depends on your current cycle status and the terms of your investment participation. Full details are provided upon account registration and cycle enrollment.

NOVEX employs multiple security layers including email verification, OTP-based two-factor authentication, and continuous AI monitoring for suspicious activity. All account actions require authentication, and investor data is protected with enterprise-grade encryption protocols.

KYC (Know Your Customer) verification is required for all NOVEX investors before accessing investment cycles. The process involves submitting a valid government-issued identity document and completing a brief verification check. This process protects all participants and ensures a trusted investor community.
